# KidQuest

**Screen-free activity ideas in seconds**

> #6 of 15 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Weekly · Easy, content-heavy · Revenue potential: Medium · Family, Pets & Plants

## What it is

Tell KidQuest your children's ages, how long you've got, indoors or out, and what's in the house, and it suggests activities with simple instructions. Save favourites and plan a week of ideas.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Parents hit this problem every weekend and school holiday, and they pay for things that save their sanity. A deep, well-organised library is the product, it works offline and needs no accounts.

## Who it's for

Parents and carers of children aged 3–12.

**The problem:** Bored kids, no plan and 30 minutes. Finding a suitable idea quickly is hard.

## Version one

- Filters: age, indoor/outdoor, time, energy, cost, materials
- Activity cards with simple steps
- Favourites and 'done today'
- Weekly activity planner
- Saved activities available offline

**Example:** A parent with 30 minutes and two bored children gets three indoor ideas using what's in the kitchen.

## Later

- Printable packs
- Seasonal packs: half-term, Christmas, summer
- Rainy-day mode

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Pick an activity: a giant dice rolls across the screen
- Done today: a sticker slaps onto the chart
- Rainy-day mode: a stickman family in raincoats jumps in puddles
- Week planned: a cardboard rocket launches
- Seasonal pack: pumpkins or snowmen roll in


## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.
